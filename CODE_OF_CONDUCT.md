# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free research community.
Participants should be considerate, discuss ideas and evidence rather than
people, accept good-faith correction, and protect privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
privacy violations, publishing another person's private information, or using
project channels for unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict contributions and
participation to protect the community and the research boundary. Concerns
should be reported privately through the repository host.
