# Excluded private surfaces

The export intentionally excludes:

- private repository history, branches, tags, remotes, review records, and
  coordination artifacts;
- hosting, deployment, infrastructure, database, migration, and account
  configuration;
- connectors, provider adapters, credentials, environment files, and connected
  execution receipts;
- user interfaces and generated build artifacts;
- private governance, approval, promotion, and acceptance machinery;
- internal evidence, historical experiments, archived roadmaps, work-package
  records, logs, screenshots, scratch material, and local caches;
- provider-specific calibration and maturity records;
- real or potentially identifying data;
- third-party datasets and proprietary product-shaped templates;
- economy and ecosystem implementations whose public support would require a
  much larger dependency and verification surface.

These categories are described without reproducing private paths, identifiers,
values, or operational details.
