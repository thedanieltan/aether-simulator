# WP-AES-04 acceptance record

## Objective

Extend the shared deterministic world kernel from enterprise ecosystems to a
synthetic economy whose aggregate indicators derive from entity-level activity.

## Delivered

- Versioned economy configuration and event-payload contracts.
- Citizens, households, firms, nonprofits, banks, government, regulators, and
  market institutions using shared world collections.
- Employment, household, firm, credit, tax, expenditure, market, formation,
  failure, shock, and intervention mechanisms.
- Seven deterministic scenarios, three canonical full exports, lifecycle
  fixtures, CLI commands, demonstration, benchmarks, ADR, and documentation.
- Partition-independent construction and assumption/outcome comparison.

## Acceptance evidence

- Same inputs produce byte-identical events and exports.
- Household income, firm counterparty transactions, bank balance sheets, and
  taxes reconcile.
- Employment, production, consumption, credit, defaults, and closures derive
  from underlying entity states and events.
- Checkpoint, resume, replay, branching, and comparison are deterministic.
- Single-batch and partitioned construction produce byte-identical worlds.
- The requested shock families appear in tested scenario events.

## Status

- Implementation: complete.
- Integration: complete through protected pull-request review and required
  checks.
- Deployment: included in the static browser scenario gallery delivered by
  WP-AES-05.
- Live acceptance: not separately performed for an economy browser journey;
  deterministic local and hosted-CI acceptance is complete.
